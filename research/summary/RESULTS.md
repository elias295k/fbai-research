# Public v1 research synthesis results

## Outcome

The programme closes with `DATA_CEILING_UPHELD`. LR52 remains the default
internal model because no advanced candidate passed its predefined development
gate. This is a decision under the frozen public protocol, not a claim that
LR52 is globally optimal.

The strongest advanced result was Match2Vec, improving aligned development Log
Loss by `0.003764`, below the `0.005` threshold. Graph SVD improved by
`0.001456`, controlled deep capacity by `0.000164`, and pseudo-xG by
`0.000039`. The player-availability and Understat-xG candidates were worse
than their identical-row LR52 comparators. Every gate verdict is therefore
`false`.

The closing market is reported separately. On its aligned nine-league
development population it beat LR52 by `0.025352` Log Loss, and on the
historical-final population by `0.021733`. It is a near-kickoff external
benchmark with different information timing, not a selectable internal
candidate and not evidence of a deployable strategy.

The combined evidence indicates that extra capacity alone was not material and
that the tested representation, xG, graph, and player-history signals were weak
or unstable. Match2Vec was closest to advancement, but remained below the
frozen threshold. The justified next direction is broader, genuinely
pre-kickoff timestamped information, not another untargeted capacity search.

See [the central research summary](../../docs/RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md) for the
complete comparison table and [the limitation statement](../../docs/RESEARCH_LIMITATIONS.md)
for the bounds of the conclusion. `result.json` is the machine-readable
authority generated by the same catalogue exercised in tests.

## Source-inventory outcome

The public v1 catalogue represents the baseline, closing benchmark, and six
advanced internal candidate families. It also records completed source work
that is intentionally not normalized here: market-aware combinations, odds
movement, odds-derived features and stacking, open candidate discovery, and
legacy model-family, tuning, clustering, interaction, and walk-forward audits.
Those are proposed for separate source-verification phases; their raw scores
must not be mixed into this synthesis.

# Research limitations

## What the closure supports

`DATA_CEILING_UPHELD` means that no advanced internal candidate in the frozen
public v1 programme met its predefined development gate against LR52 on
identical rows. It justifies retaining LR52 as the default for this repository.

## What it does not prove

- It does not prove that LR52 is globally optimal or that no future model can
  improve football outcome probabilities.
- It does not prove that the closing market cannot be approached.
- It does not establish causal effects, betting profitability, operational
  utility, live-data reliability, or deployment readiness.
- It does not make the examined 2025 historical-final folds unseen again.

## Evidence constraints

The work is observational historical evaluation. Candidate populations differ
because Understat, graph, and player records cover narrower divisions and
seasons; only within-experiment, identical-row improvements are comparable.
Raw Log Loss is never ranked across those populations.

Post-match participation, lineup, match-statistic, and xG observations are
used only as lagged completed-match history. They are not same-match predictors.
Broad timestamped pre-kickoff availability is incomplete, and target official
lineups and bench membership were excluded where publication time could not be
verified. External xG coverage also ends before the full canonical history.

Closing market prices reflect later, near-kickoff information than the internal
feature set. Their advantage is an external information benchmark, not an
apples-to-apples candidate comparison and not a forecast-profit claim.

All public historical-final results have now been examined. Any future model
selection requires a genuinely new untouched temporal final set. Until then,
additional retrospective improvements would be development evidence only.
